## Inputs
- `[WORD]`

## Build
- Heavy condensed bold, white, ~330 px tall, centred behind the head, fully inside x 120–960 (shrink to fit). The head must occlude part of it.

## Timing (90 frames)
| Frames | What |
|---|---|
| 0–10 | Rises from lower, blur→sharp, transparent→solid |
| 10–75 | Hold with slow ~20 px lift |
| 75–90 | Drops back down and fades |

## Variants (source: vibha-51)
- **huge-strike**: one huge cream serif word fitted to 90% width wipes in left→right behind the head (12 frames). At ~18 frames a slightly wobbly red marker line crosses it out (5 frames).
- **crayon-write**: a red handwritten word is revealed left→right as if written (24 frames), with a small red crayon riding the leading edge. Then a wobbly underline, and the crayon flies off down-right.
- **red-collage**: the person goes black and white, a giant red word sits behind, and three short lines slam in beside you.
